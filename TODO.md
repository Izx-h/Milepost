# Website outcomes

- [x] **Mobile-first public importer website:** Provide a responsive public landing experience for Kenyan buyers looking to import Japanese-auction vehicles. Explain the service as car sourcing and importing rather than a car-yard inventory site.
- [x] **Import process and staged costs:** Explain, in order, customer request and bidding, the KES 300,000 bidding payment, the payment for the won vehicle and shipping, arrival in Mombasa, separate duty/clearance payment, and collection or delivery. Clearly distinguish the deposit from later amounts. Do not invent final refund terms or payment deadlines; state that exact terms are confirmed in the signed mandate/quote.
- [x] **Buyer trust and sample proof:** Show the documents and status evidence a buyer should expect through the process, plus service assurances. Any sample vehicle/order evidence or testimonial must be clearly labelled illustrative/sample and not presented as a real customer review.
- [x] **Illustrative vehicle journey:** Provide a sample car journey page or component with visible, ordered milestones from bidding through export/shipping, Mombasa clearance and delivery. Label all sample data as illustrative; do not suggest it is a live customer order.
- [x] **Indicative landed-cost estimator:** Let visitors enter sample vehicle and shipping/cost details and see a live itemized indicative total. Show that duty is an estimate and the final amount depends on KRA's current valuation and applicable tariffs. Do not present the output as a quote or actual payment due. Show the supplied approximately KES 100,000 commission as an editable example service-fee line, and the supplied approximately 25% car-yard saving only as a non-guaranteed comparison; invite a like-for-like written price check.
- [x] **WhatsApp-first vehicle request:** Provide a usable vehicle-request/enquiry form that assembles a structured message with the buyer's requirements. It must not send users to a made-up business phone number; if no business number is configured, provide a copy/share fallback and clearly indicate the number must be supplied before WhatsApp handoff.
- [x] **Blade/Vue implementation:** Render public copy and route content in Laravel Blade. Use Vue.js components for the interactive estimator, sample journey experience, and enquiry/message preparation. No real payments, database-backed order status, customer login, live order data, or external API integrations in this frontend demonstration.
